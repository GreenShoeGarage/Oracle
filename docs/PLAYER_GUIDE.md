# ORACLE player guide

ORACLE · LARP Field Kit carries your event briefing, character, and discoveries. Your organizer prepares the story; you use the app for short actions while playing with the people and props around you.

This guide describes v1.1.0. The [release status](STATUS.md) records verification and deployment. Your organizer chooses which instruments and character options appear.

## Join and choose your character

1. Open [oracle.greenshoegarage.com](https://oracle.greenshoegarage.com) and create an account or sign in. Your account belongs to you; characters belong to particular events. A new account does not automatically join an event.
2. On **Your events**, choose **Enter invitation code** and enter the event code your organizer supplied. If you already have events, expand **Join or organize another event**. Spaces and hyphens in the code are accepted.
3. Open the event, read its briefing, then choose **Characters**. If your organizer assigned you a prewritten sheet, open it and read your private goals.
4. If creation is allowed, choose **Create character** and work through **Identity → Abilities → Kit & goals → Review**. Save the sheet, open it, then select **Submit for approval**. If the event does not require approval, use **Activate character** instead.
5. Check for approval or private feedback. An approved character assigned to you is needed for live play. Editing an approved sheet returns it to Draft; submit or activate it again before playing. Ask the organizer if creation is disabled or you have reached the character limit.
6. Choose **Open adventure**, select your approved character, then **Scan a prop** or type its printed code. Your organizer must have started Rehearsal or Live play before new game actions work.

The public badge always includes an approved character's name and may include other fields chosen by the organizer. Private objectives, attributes, equipment, inventory, and review notes are excluded. Your complete sheet is visible to you and authorized event/project managers. TRACE private investigations have a separate privacy boundary; managers cannot open them merely because they manage the event.

## Use the right code

| Code | Where to use it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event invitation | **Enter invitation code** on Your events | Adds your account to the organizer's event. |
| Character badge | **Scan a badge** in Characters | Shows an approved public identity to another signed-in member of the same event. It grants no control of that character. |
| Prop label | **Scan a prop** in Open adventure, or the relevant instrument's code entry | Finds an event instrument for your selected character. Protected content still needs its conditions. |
| Temporary exchange invitation | **Scan player** in Exchanges | Opens a proposed introduction or exchange for both players to review. It expires 15 minutes after creation. |

A DEAD DROP release word is another, separate clue. Scanning its prop label does not supply the secret word. Camera scanning is optional: use the printed code, supported ORACLE link, or a QR image in the relevant scanner. **Start camera** asks for permission; closing or stopping the scanner ends camera use.

## Meet someone and exchange selected information

Both players need their own approved characters in the same Live or Rehearsal event and an online connection for the final agreement.

1. Open **Exchanges** and select your character. One player chooses **Show my QR**; the other chooses **Scan player**, scans that temporary QR or enters its 12-character code, then selects **Join exchange**.
2. Choose the readings you intend to share and save your offer. With BAZAAR enabled you can separately offer owned item quantities and fictional resources. Leave both offers empty for an introduction only.
3. Review both public identities and every offered title, item, and resource quantity. The other player's reading text/audio is hidden until completion.
4. Each player selects **Confirm this exchange** for the same saved revision. Changing either offer clears both confirmations. Completion is established only when ORACLE reports **Exchange complete** and supplies the receipt.

A shared reading becomes a journal copy; it does not complete a discovery or grant skills or flags. Item/resource transfers happen only when explicitly included in the accepted terms. All agreed transfers succeed together or none do. A badge scan, joining an exchange, or one player's confirmation does not authorize a transfer.

If the response is uncertain, use the app's explicit retry for that same request. Do not start a replacement trade to guess whether the first one worked. Refresh and review current terms if a partner, policy, resource, or inventory changed. Either participant may cancel an open exchange; the invited player may reject it.

## What the twelve instruments do

Use **More tools for this event** for tools outside the main adventure. Availability depends on your event, character, and current conditions.

| Instrument | Your action |
| --- | --- |
| RELIC | Scan/type a prop code and choose an available examination; its reading enters your journal. |
| DEAD DROP | Follow the clue, supply the separate release word when required, and open the message. Its transcript contains the required information without audio. |
| CIPHERBOX | Submit an answer, request an available hint, and follow the recorded success or exhausted-attempt result. |
| WAYFINDER | Review a scene's conditions, location, time, and available places; join when eligible. Linked scenes require a STAGEHAND assignment. |
| TRACE | Under **Investigations · TRACE**, save evidence, people, places, or theories. Records start private; deliberately choose an audience to share your written notes. Citations alone do not share readings. |
| WHISPER | Open **Rumors & news**, review an eligible rumor and source, then **Collect** it. It remains an unverified telling. |
| BROADSIDE | Read approved news in **Rumors & news**. You may propose a bulletin; it becomes visible only after organizer review and publication. |
| BAZAAR | Open **Shops & balances · BAZAAR**, check current prices and funds, and buy available stock. Use Exchanges for bilateral barter. All resources are fictional. |
| OATHBOOK | Open **Agreements · OATHBOOK**, review the exact terms, and explicitly accept. Every participant separately **Confirm fulfillment** to settle eligible terms. A revision clears earlier signatures. |
| SIGIL | Open **Cooperative challenges · SIGIL** and gather around the host device. Review its components, name the in-person roles, and follow ordered checkpoints. Pause before stepping away. |
| STATIC | Read an available fictional prop/zone state and collect it if you want a historical journal copy. An old reading does not establish the current state. |
| STAGEHAND | Open **Parties & scenes · STAGEHAND**, queue when allowed, and accept your current assignment. Wait for staff dispatch and check in on return. Queueing alone reserves no place. |

## Prepare your device and keep notes

Load ORACLE while connected before going into the field. Expand **Install ORACLE** for the supported browser's install command or instructions. On iPhone/iPad, the app's guidance uses Safari's **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Installation wording and support vary; you can continue in the browser when installation is unavailable.

The install panel reports whether the public app interface is cached. Prepare your event material separately:

1. Open your event, then **Field desk**, while signed in and connected. Preparation defaults to the event you opened. If several events are available, choose **Event to prepare** and select **Use this event**. Check the event name above the preparation button. This choice is separate from the character scope used for notes and requests.
2. Select **Prepare for the field**. ORACLE downloads the player briefing and rules, your own approved character sheets, and already revealed journal entries. It checks that saved material can be read back from this device.
3. Expand the event under the preparation panel. Check the saved time and any **Preparation needs attention** messages. Briefing/rules can be saved before a character is approved, but the missing-character notice remains until you have an approved sheet and prepare again. Failed journal downloads and blocked/full storage also need attention before leaving connectivity. The app-shell status is shown separately.
4. Open **Player briefing**, **Event rules**, and **Your character** to inspect the saved references. Select **Read saved journal entries** to check the saved text/audio. Return here after disconnecting to read the same material. Prepare again while connected when the organizer changes the event or your character.

Character references include your biography, abilities, private objectives, starting equipment, and inventory when prepared. They exclude other players, staff/review notes, and badge credentials. Inventory is a dated reference; it cannot prove current ownership or authorize a transfer. Briefing references exclude organizer-only material and unrevealed instrument answers.

Preparation does not save live scenes, timers, shops, balances, trade terms, investigations, or staff operations. Storage restrictions, clearing browser data, an oversized preparation, or an oversized journal can prevent a usable local copy. A saved confirmation describes this browser/device, not another phone or an independent backup. Actual iPhone/Android airplane-mode cold starts remain part of the device acceptance work recorded in [PILOT.md](PILOT.md).

For a durable local note:

1. Open **Field desk** while connected and select **Check my characters in this event**.
2. Choose your approved character, enter the note, and select **Save note on this device**. Wait for a saved status; a reported storage failure means it was not saved.
3. Return to Field desk to read or edit that saved note. It belongs to this browser/device and does not synchronize to another phone, update your online journal, or change your character.

Other unsaved forms remain in the open page. A navigation warning gives you a chance to keep them, but a tab closing or reloading can still lose unsaved text. When two tabs edit the same saved Field desk note, review the conflict before deciding which text to keep.

## Review pending work after reconnecting

Field desk can explicitly save an invitation request, a request to join a temporary exchange, or a reading-only offer from an opened exchange. It does not queue items/resources, confirmations, protected discoveries, puzzle answers, scene admissions, timers, or staff actions. Nothing sends automatically when connectivity returns.

| State or action | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Saved on this device; it has not established an online invitation, offer, or exchange. |
| Uncertain | A response was lost or incomplete; the server may already have received the request. |
| Review needed | Current access, age, expiry, policy, or terms must be checked before proceeding. |
| Confirmed request | That individual server request succeeded. Both players may still need to confirm the exchange online. |
| **Discard unsent request** | Removes a request before its first transmission. |
| **Stop local retries** | Stops this device from retrying an attempted request; it does not cancel a server action. |

Choose **Review and send**, inspect and acknowledge the exact request, then **Check access and send**. For uncertainty, use **Review exact retry**. ORACLE rechecks your original account, current event/character access, policy, and terms. Requests older than 24 hours need review; server invitations can expire sooner. Never change an uncertain request's meaning to try to force it through.

Offline readings and notes cannot sign you in or prove current permissions, balances, availability, or timer results. Use **Reconnect to ORACLE** and review the live state before continuing play. For SIGIL, reconnect and explicitly resume a paused attempt; the device cannot declare success by itself.

## Updates and shared devices

When an update is ready, save Field desk notes and finish or preserve other open work first. Use **Apply update and reload this tab** when your group can pause. If older tabs block it, finish and close them, then apply again. A compatible update preserves supported saved device data; it does not automatically save unsaved form text or force other active tabs to reload.

Version 1.1 upgrades this browser's Field desk storage while keeping saved contexts, notes, and requests. Older ORACLE versions can no longer write to that upgraded store. If an older tab reports that local storage needs an update, save or copy any open unsaved text, then close or update that tab. This browser update does not change the live event database.

Sign out before handing a device to another person. Signing out or switching accounts clears local private scope. Leaving an event clears its prepared references, readings, notes, and queued requests. **Clear device data** in Field desk and **Clear all local data** in Saved readings both delete prepared kits, saved readings, notes, and requests from this device after confirmation. They do not undo already received server requests or delete the live event.

During a disconnection the app cannot learn that an organizer changed your access. Previously saved material is historical; protect the device accordingly. For blocked actions, installation, cameras, or failed saves, see [troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
